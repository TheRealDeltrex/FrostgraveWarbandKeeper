Chronomancer (9)
Spell Base CN Type
Crumble 10 Line of Sight Info

This spell can only target inanimate structures such as buildings and walls. The spellcaster rapidly speeds up the passing of time in a small area of the structure, causing it to collapse. This can create a doorway-sized hole through any wall, which should be indicated on the table somehow. The spell can also be used to collapse a section of floor beneath a figure standing on a level above the ground. In this case, the figure about to be affected must pass a Move Roll (TN22) or fall to the next level down and taking damage appropriately. If this spell is cast on a wall created by the Wall spell, the wall is completely destroyed. If it is cast on terrain holding a Wizard Eye, the Wizard Eye is cancelled.

Decay 12 Line of Sight Info

The spellcaster selects and attacks a target’s weapon, causing it to decay and fall apart, rendering it useless for the rest of the game. This spell has no effect on magic weapons (even those only temporarily enchanted). This spell has no effect on creatures (unless they are specifically identified as being equipped with a weapon from the General Arms and Armour List).

Fast Act 8 Line of Sight Info

This spell may only be cast on a member of the spellcaster’s warband or an uncontrolled creature. This figure will activate at the end of the current phase instead of in its normal phase. For example, if an apprentice casts this spell on an uncontrolled creature, the creature will activate at the end of that player’s Apprentice phase instead of the Creature phase. Spellcasters may not cast this spell on themselves, nor on a figure that has already activated in the current turn.

Fleet Feet 10 Line of Sight Info

The target receives +2 Move for the rest of the game. Multiple castings of Fleet Feet on the same target have no effect.

Petrify 10 Line of Sight Info

The target figure must make an immediate Will Roll with a Target Number equal to the Casting Roll. If it fails, it receives no actions in its next activation. Furthermore, the figure suffers -3 Fight (to a minimum of +0) and may not have Leap cast upon it until after it makes its next move action. Large creatures receive +8 to their Will Roll to resist this spell.

Slow 10 Line of Sight Info

The target is reduced to a maximum of one action per activation (which can be any action, it does not have to be movement). It may make an Will Roll verses the Casting Roll at the end of each of its activations. If successful the spell is cancelled.

Slowfall Into the Breeding Pits 8 Reaction / Line of Sight Info

This spell allows the target to fall any distance and take no damage. It may be cast any time a figure falls within line of sight of the spellcaster (including the spellcaster himself), or during the spellcaster's own activation as a regular Line of Sight spell targeting a figure about to fall in its next activation — in that case the distance fallen counts against the figure's movement allowance for the activation.

Time Store 14 Self Only Info

The spellcaster captures a fragment of their own present to save for future use. To cast this spell, the spellcaster must be able to take two actions during their activation. They must spend the first action casting Time Store. If successful, the second action is lost. This spellcaster is now considered to have a stored ‘extra action’ that they may use in a future turn. This action can only be used during the spellcaster’s activation and can give the spellcaster three actions in one activation.

Time Walk 14 Self Only Info

Wizard only. The wizard will activate again in the Apprentice phase and the Soldier phase. This is in addition to the figures that can normally activate in those phases. The wizard may not activate any additional soldiers or be part of a group activation in these phases. The wizard may perform one action in each of these two phases and may take any action – they are not limited to movement. If the wizard moved at all in a previous activation during the turn, any additional move actions are at half rate. If a wizard casts this spell in consecutive turns, they immediately suffer 8 points of damage. CHRONOMANCER

Call Storm 12 Area Effect Info

All bow and crossbow attacks are made with -1 Shoot for the rest of the game. This spell may be cast multiple times (and by multiple spellcasters), with each additional casting increasing the penalty by a further -1, up to a maximum of -5.

Destructive Sphere 12 Area Effect Info

Every figure within 3” of the spellcaster (but not counting the spellcaster) suffers a +5 elemental magic attack.

The spellcaster selects an enemy figure within 16” and line of sight and hurls a ball of destructive elemental energy at it. The target and every figure within 1” and line of sight of the target immediately suffers a +5 elemental magic shooting attack. Roll this shooting attack separately for each figure. This spell may not target an enemy figure that is even partially obscured by another figure.

Elemental Bolt 12 Line of Sight Info

The spellcaster makes a +7 elemental magic shooting attack against a target figure within 16” and line of sight.

Elemental Hammer 10 Line of Sight Info

This spell is cast upon a weapon. The next time the figure wielding this weapon wins a round of combat and does at least 1 point of damage, this weapon inflicts an additional 5 points of elemental magic damage. If cast on a normal weapon, which is then used against a creature that is Immune to Normal Weapons, this weapon will only deal the 5 points of elemental magic damage. If cast on a bow or crossbow the spell only applies to the next attack.

Elemental Lash Into the Breeding Pits 12 Reaction Info

This spell may be cast whenever an enemy figure comes within 1" of the spellcaster. The caster makes a +3 shooting attack against the target. If the spell does at least one point of damage, the target is moved 1" directly away from the caster, its movement ends, and any additional actions it had are lost.

Elemental Shield 10 Self Only Info

The spellcaster forms a floating shield that absorbs the next 3 points of damage the spellcaster would normally suffer in combat or from a shooting attack. Once 3 points have been absorbed the spell is cancelled. A spellcaster may only have one Elemental Shield active at any time.

Scatter Shot 12 Area Effect Info

The spellcaster makes a +0 elemental magic shooting attack against every enemy figure (either from an opposing warband or uncontrolled creature) within 12” and line of sight. This may include enemy figures in combat, although the normal rules for shooting into combat are followed in this case.

Wall 10 Line of Sight Info

This spell creates a 6”-long, 3”-high wall, part of which must be within 10” and line of sight of the spellcaster. This wall can be climbed as normal. At the end of each turn, after the turn in which the spell was cast, roll a die, on a 1–4 the wall vanishes. ELEMENTALIST

Enchanter (8)
Spell Base CN Type
Animate Construct 10 Out of Game (B) Info

It is assumed that the spellcaster has built a construct prior to using this spell to animate it. If the spell is successfully cast, the construct immediately becomes a permanent member of the warband, taking the place of a soldier. A spellcaster must declare the size of construct they are attempting to animate (small, medium, or large – page 183 before rolling to cast the spell. The larger the construct, the harder it is to animate, so the following modifiers are applied to the Casting Roll: Small -0, Medium -3, Large -6. There is no limit to the number of constructs in a warband other than the normal limits for soldiers. Large constructs count as specialist soldiers, the others as standard soldiers.

Control Construct 12 Line of Sight Info

The target construct must make an immediate Will Roll with a Target Number equal to the Casting Roll. If the roll fails, the construct becomes a temporary member of the spellcaster’s warband. This control lasts for the rest of the game or until the spell is cancelled. The spellcaster may spend an action to cancel this spell. A spellcaster may only control one construct at a time.

Embed Enchantment 14 Out of Game (A) Info

This spell causes any one Enchant Armour or Enchant Weapon spell that is still active at the end of a game to become permanent, and the weapon or armour in question to become a magic weapon or armour. The newly created magic weapon or armour takes up an item slot as normal.

Enchant Armour 8 Line of Sight Info

This spell may only be cast on a figure wearing armour. The armour worn by the target now counts as magic armour and grants +1 Armour for the rest of the game. Multiple castings of this spell on the same target have no effect.

Enchant Weapon 8 Line of Sight Info

This spell targets a weapon of the spellcaster’s choosing. If cast on a melee weapon, this weapon counts as a magic weapon with +1 Fight. Bows and crossbows count as magic weapons with +1 Shoot, but the attacks made with them do not count as magic attacks. This spell may be cast on a single arrow or crossbow bolt, in which case that ammunition gives +1 Shoot and its attack counts as magic, but for the next shooting attack only. This spell may only be cast once on each weapon. When using both a magic missile weapon and magic ammunition, the shooter may choose to apply the bonus of one or the other, but not both.

Grenade 10 Line of Sight Info

The spellcaster takes an object, commonly a simple rock, imbues it with magic energy and throws it at their target, whereupon it explodes into hundreds of fragments. The spellcaster picks a target point within 14”. Every figure, including allies, within 1.5” of that point immediately suffers a +3 magic shooting attack. Use the target point as the origin of the attack for working out line of sight and cover.

Strength 10 Line of Sight Info

The target receives +2 Fight. Multiple Strength spells on the same target have no effect.

Telekinesis 10 Line of Sight Info

The spellcaster may move any treasure token within 16” by up to 6” in any direction, so long as it remains in line of sight the entire time. If the treasure moves out of line of sight, it immediately falls straight to the ground. This spell has no effect on a treasure token that has any special requirements for how and when it can be picked up, nor on one being carried by a figure. This spell may not target the central treasure, until after that treasure has been picked up for the first time. ENCHANTER

A skeletal hand reaches out of the ground and grabs the target’s ankle. The figure may not take any move actions until it escapes. Any form of magic movement, except the Leap spell, allows a figure to escape the hand; otherwise, the only way to escape is to fight the hand, which has Fight +0, Health 1. If the hand takes one point of damage, it vanishes, and the target is free. Other figures in base contact may attack the hand or give a support bonus. If the hand wins the fight, it does damage as normal. This spell may only be cast against a target that is standing on the ground. Large creatures are unaffected by this spell. Lichdom Thaw of the Lich Lord 20 Out of Game Info

This spell may only be cast by a wizard. It is a dramatic and horrifying process wherein the wizard tears his soul from his body, animates his own lifeless corpse, and returns his soul to his new undead form. A wizard that successfully casts Lichdom immediately becomes a lich. Failure scales with how badly the casting roll was missed, from -1 level and -1 Health up to the wizard's soul being lost forever on a miss of 21+. As a lich, each level costs 150 XP, Fight and Shoot can never be raised on level-up, Will caps at +10 and Health at 25, and the lich is immune to Control Undead, Poison Dart, Restore Life and the Elixir of Life. Liches may not cast Familiar or Transcendence, may cast Heal/Miraculous Cure/Restore Life only on undead, and may never have a rangifer join their warband.

Raise Zombie 10 Out of Game (B) / Touch Info

The spellcaster adds one zombie (page 193) to their warband as a temporary member. If the spell is cast before the game, the zombie can be deployed normally. If it is cast during a game, the zombie appears in base contact with the spellcaster. A warband may only have one raised zombie at any one time. If the zombie is killed or exits the table, Raise Zombie can be cast again to create another.

Revenant Thaw of the Lich Lord 14 Out of Game Info

This spell may be cast after any game in which a soldier in the spellcaster's warband was killed. The spellcaster makes the casting roll on a selected dead warband member; on success the soldier is reanimated as a revenant, keeping all its stats from life except Will, which becomes +0. The revenant is undead and continues to wield whatever weapons and armour it died with. If a revenant is reduced to 0 Health, Revenant may be cast on it again after the game to restore it to full Health. There is no limit on the number of revenants a warband may contain. Revenant may not be cast on undead (other than revenants), constructs, or demons.

Spell Eater 12 Line of Sight Info

Casting this spell causes the spellcaster to immediately take 1 point of damage. When this spell is cast, it cancels the effects of a single casting of any one spell currently in play. This spell cannot unsummon a creature, but it can cancel the control of a creature.

Steal Health 10 Line of Sight Info

The target must make an immediate Will Roll with a Target Number equal to the Casting Roll. If failed, the target immediately loses 3 Health and the spellcaster regains 3 Health. The spellcaster gains 3 Health, even if the target had less Health than that remaining. This may not take the spellcaster above their starting Health. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs. A spellcaster may target a member of their own warband – if they do, however, the target immediately (and permanently) leaves the warband and is treated as an uncontrolled creature for the rest of the game.

Strike Dead 18 Line of Sight Info

This spell targets a figure within 8”. The target must make a Will Roll with a Target Number equal to the Casting Roll or be immediately reduced to 0 Health. All figures may empower their Will Roll to resist this spell, even non-spellcasters. The spellcaster immediately loses 1 Health upon attempting this spell (even if it is cast successfully), in addition to any loss incurred by failure or empowerment. This spell has no effect on undead or constructs. NECROMANCER

The Perilous Dark

Solo & co-op toolkit

Also codifies house style for "boss monsters" (make them multi-step puzzles, not just high stat-lines — and make them immune to Mind Control) and gives explicit guidance that solo/co-op tables should run less crowded than a normal Frostgrave table, since the simple creature AI can't exploit terrain the way a player can.

Warband building
Solo: build a warband as normal. Co-op: each player's wizard gets only 200gc and a max of 4 soldiers, so two players together land close to a solo warband's power.

Objectives over treasure
Place only 2 treasure tokens solo (3 co-op) in hard-to-reach spots, and shift most of the XP budget onto scenario-specific goals (e.g. "+80 XP if the statue is destroyed") to keep games from becoming a pure gold-count.

New turn sequence
Wizard Phase → Creature Phase I (creatures with Fight +5 or Health 20+) → Apprentice Phase → Creature Phase II (everyone else) → Soldier Phase — lets dangerous monsters act before the party's own soldiers.

Updated creature AI
Creatures activate in current-Health order, always move to engage the highest-level spellcaster if one move can do it, never intentionally pick a multi-combat, and all creatures get a flat +2 Will.

Limited Duration spells
Circle of Protection, Invisibility, Monstrous Form, and Wall: roll a die at the end of any turn they're active — on a 1–10 the spell ends early (replaces Wall's normal end-of-turn roll).

Target Points
Instead of random movement, uncontrolled creatures head for a designer-nominated point (usually the objective) — makes the AI feel purposeful instead of aimless.

Monster spawning
Bespoke per-scenario spawn tables (not the core random-encounter table) tied to numbered spawn points, so the designer controls both what shows up and where.

Clue Markers
Treasure-token-like markers that reward investigation (an action + a roll on a scenario-specific clue table) — the game's investigation/mystery layer.

Room Cards
Draw a card to set up the next room only when a door is opened, so players never see the whole table at once; can also define room shape/doorway count for a lightweight dungeon crawl.

Closely Connected Scenarios
Rules for chaining scenarios back-to-back without a return to base: partial healing (+6 or half Health), no buying/selling/hiring, one Out of Game spell each.

Dungeon crawl systems

Two full worked examples ship with the book: The Vaults (a fixed 10-card dungeon deck with two objective rooms — a treasure hoard and a stairway down) and Dungeons Deep (the fully randomly-generated version, with its own room/encounter/treasure tables and the Gateway Home rule in action).

Dungeon Cards
Like Room Cards but smaller and more numerous (10–12 sections instead of 3–5 rooms); the deck is split into a "multi-exit" pile and an "objective room" pile, then interleaved so objectives can't appear too early.

Randomly Generated Dungeon
Each new room triggers three rolls — a room table, an encounter table, and a treasure/clue table — for a fully improvised dungeon; includes a Will TN20 "search for a secret door" rule to rescue dead-ended layouts.

Gateway Room / Home
The randomly generated dungeon's designated exit: only spawns once 6+ rooms are revealed, guarded by a double encounter roll, and is the sole way off the table.

New core mechanics

Turn order is Heritor phase → Warden phase → Crew phase → Creature phase (3-step AI: fight if engaged, shoot/close on the nearest visible foe, else random move). Combat/shooting math mirrors core Frostgrave closely (Fight/Shoot rolls, cover, push-back), with an optional Critical Hit rule (natural 20 auto-wins and doubles damage) and an optional Wounded rule (≤4 Health = 1 action & -2 to all rolls).

Crew composition
Max 10: 1 Heritor + 1 Warden (mandatory, only one ever per crew) + up to 8 crewmen (max 4 paid Specialists). All rolls use d20.

Building a Heritor
Picks 5 of 30 shared Abilities (one at Utilization Number 3, four at UN5); may raise one non-Armour stat by +1; 5 item slots.

Utilization Rolls & Blood Burn (signature new subsystem)
Using a Heritor Ability costs 1 self-damage ("Blood Burn") to attempt, +2 more on a failed d20-vs-UN roll. Extra attempts the same turn stack a penalty to the UN: +0/+3/+6/+9/+12/+15 for the 1st–6th attempt — a practical cap of ~6/turn.

Building a Warden
Base M6 F+1 S+0 A10 W+4 H14; picks a Branch (below); learns 4 starting spells (3 in-Branch, 1 out-of-Branch at +4 Casting Number); 3 item slots; can never wear armour or a shield.

Split Stats
Notation like "+2/+3": first number is the true/base stat for leveling, second is the current effective stat for rolls.

Empowering & resisting spells
A Warden can empower a cast (or her own resistance roll) by trading Health 1-for-1, even to the point of a killing cast. Targets resist vs. the higher of the final Casting Roll or 14.

Swimming Rolls
Activating in deep water needs a Will roll (TN5, modified by armour/shield/treasure); fail = no activation + damage. Amphibious/Aquatic traits bypass it entirely.

Small Boats
Capacity 6, moves 2"+1"/rider (max 6"), -1 Fight/Shoot aboard, boarding needs a Fight roll; can be attacked (Fight+0/Armour14/Health30).

Map Stones (campaign win condition)
10 unique, unsellable clue-items toward the legendary Crystal Pool; +20 XP each; collecting all 10 is the stated campaign endgame.

New Recruits
If only the Heritor dies, the Warden's level sets the replacement (capped gains); if only the Warden dies, it's the Heritor's level ÷3. Both dying disbands the crew.
