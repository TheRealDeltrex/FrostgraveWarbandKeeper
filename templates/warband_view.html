Additional Rules and Homerules

Optional house rules and supplement content, off by default (not part of core 2e). Turn on a source book to make its soldiers available to this warband; the captain / soldier-leveling options below are adapted from the FG1E Sellswords supplement — tune these to match your group's own version.

Source books

Switch on the supplements you play with. Enabled books add their mercenaries to the Hire soldier list. Every creature is always browsable in the Frostgrave Lexicon.

{% for book in source_books %} {% endfor %}

Needs The Maze of Malcor. Its five lost schools are scroll-only by the book; this is the optional rule for running them as real schools. Only affects newly created warbands — a wizard's own school is fixed once chosen.

Needs Blood Legacy. The book frames the Fire Giant Wizard as a build for very hard/large encounters rather than balanced campaign play — this opts it in as a choosable wizard school. Only affects newly created warbands — a wizard's own school is fixed once chosen.

Needs Blood Legacy. The book frames the Vampire Wizard as a GM/NPC villain build "or PC by group agreement" — this opts it in as a choosable wizard school. Only affects newly created warbands — a wizard's own school is fixed once chosen.

Needs Blood Legacy. Lets one ordinary soldier in the warband become Giant-Blooded (+50gc, -1 Move, -2 Will, +2 Health) — picked from a single control on the Soldiers card below rather than a button on every soldier's row, since only one may take it.

Needs The Red King. Adds a "Fill roster" control to the Soldiers card below, rolling random recruits up to 10 members and bypassing the usual gold/roster caps, per the book's endgame campaign mode. The surrounding restrictions (no spending gold, no hiring/dismissing outside this roll, no Vault storage, a soldier lost on a 1-8 survival roll) are shown there as reminder text — this app doesn't enforce them for you. Switching this on also switches on "Ordinary soldiers can take permanent injuries" below, since the Random Recruit Status Table can inflict one on any recruited soldier.

Assassin, Demon Hunter, Monk and Mystic Warrior read as costed closer to 1st edition than the rest of the 2e tables. On by default, this bumps their hiring cost (Assassin 100gc, Demon Hunter/Monk/Mystic Warrior 125gc). Applies to future hires only.

Needs Spellcaster Magazine switched on above. Off by default; the magazine's troop stat lines read as unbalanced to some groups — tick this to opt in to hiring them, while its spells, treasure and bestiary entries stay available either way.

Needs Spellcaster Magazine switched on above. On by default. When hiring a Knight or Templar, lets you pick an Order that trades 1 point of Fight or Health for a special ability — adds an order picker to their row in Hire soldier below.

Roster caps

High-Level Wizards (Blood Legacy)

Needs Blood Legacy switched on above. The book treats each of these as its own optional rule to agree on separately, so each has its own toggle here rather than one bundled switch.

+1 specialist slot per full 20 wizard levels (5 at level 20, up to 8 at level 80+).

Wizard and apprentice each gain +1 item slot at level 30, +1 more at level 70 (max +2).

Wizard's maximum potential Health Stat rises +1 per full 10 levels (max +10, so 30 at level 100+). Stacks on top of any Health cap from another wizard state the same way.

A level 100+ wizard may improve a Casting Number down to 4 instead of the normal floor of 5.

Adds a "Spend XP" option on the Wizard card for trading banked XP for gold or a one-off bonus instead of a level — no level requirement of its own, unlike the other four.

Blood Legacy's Fire Giant Wizard: Large, Strong, Elemental Resistance (2). No armour/ shield item slots, no apprentice.
