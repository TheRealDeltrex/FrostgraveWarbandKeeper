Additional Rules and Homerules

Optional house rules and supplement content, off by default (not part of core 2e). Turn on a source book to make its soldiers available to this warband; the captain / soldier-leveling options below are adapted from the FG1E Sellswords supplement — tune these to match your group's own version.

General Rules

Switch on the supplements you play with. Enabled books add their mercenaries to the Hire soldier list. Every creature is always browsable in the Frostgrave Lexicon.

Source books
{% for book in source_books %} {% endfor %}
Blood Legacy

Needs Blood Legacy switched on in Source books.

Fire Giant Wizard playable

The book frames the Fire Giant Wizard as a build for very hard/large encounters rather than balanced campaign play — this opts it in as a choosable wizard school. Only affects newly created warbands — a wizard's own school is fixed once chosen.

Vampire Wizard playable

The book frames the Vampire Wizard as a GM/NPC villain build "or PC by group agreement" — this opts it in as a choosable wizard school. Only affects newly created warbands — a wizard's own school is fixed once chosen.

Giant-Blooded soldier modification

Lets one ordinary soldier in the warband become Giant-Blooded (+50gc, -1 Move, -2 Will, +2 Health) — picked from a single control on the Soldiers card below rather than a button on every soldier's row, since only one may take it.

The Grimoire of Fin Dalka is found/bought like any other magic item (add it via the Treasury, Vault and Workshop card's Vault items) — once owned, deciphering its 8 Fire Giant spells and selling it are also done there.

High-Level Wizards

The book treats each of these as its own optional rule to agree on separately, so each has its own toggle here rather than one bundled switch.

Increased Specialist Soldier Allowance

+1 specialist slot per full 20 wizard levels (5 at level 20, up to 8 at level 80+).

Increased Item Slots

Wizard and apprentice each gain +1 item slot at level 30, +1 more at level 70 (max +2).

Increased Maximum Health

Wizard's maximum potential Health Stat rises +1 per full 10 levels (max +10, so 30 at level 100+). Stacks on top of any Health cap from another wizard state the same way.

Lower Casting Number Minimum

A level 100+ wizard may improve a Casting Number down to 4 instead of the normal floor of 5.

Alternate Experience Point Expenditure

Adds a "Spend XP" option on the Wizard card for trading banked XP for gold or a one-off bonus instead of a level — no level requirement of its own, unlike the other four.

Spellcaster Magazine

Needs Spellcaster Magazine switched on in Source books. All on by default.

Allow its soldiers (non-Legendary)

Some groups find the magazine's troop stat lines unbalanced — untick to drop just its hireable soldiers while its spells, treasure and bestiary entries stay available either way. Covers every Spellcaster Magazine soldier except Legendary Soldiers (its own toggle below).

Allow Legendary Soldiers

Legendary Soldiers (Issue 4) are a separate, more expensive troop category limited by wizard level rather than freely hired (1 base, +1 per 10 full wizard levels, max 8) — see the Legendary Soldiers limit bar in the roster once enabled. Fielding one caps ordinary Specialists at 3. Also makes the Captain a Legendary Soldier, subject to the same limit instead of the Specialist cap.

Firearms Rules

Needs "Allow its soldiers" above also switched on. Governs whether the Musketeer, Coachman and Duellist appear in Hire soldier — both toggles must be on for them to show up. Also gates the standalone Pistol/Musket/Blunderbuss items and their upgrades: switch this off and the Workshop — Firearms panel disappears, and any already in item slots stay put but can't be bought or upgraded again until it's back on.

Knightly Orders

When hiring a Knight or Templar, lets you pick an Order that trades 1 point of Fight or Health for a special ability — adds an order picker to their row in Hire soldier below.

Monster Hunting: For Fun and Profit (Issue 5)

Adds a per-game kill log to "After the game" below: record a kill for its Master Monster Table XP, then claim its prize (sold for gold, or a spell/potion component for the wizard or apprentice). See the Lexicon for the full 92-row table plus "Other Creature", a catch-all for anything not on it.

Replace Edition 1 Loot with Gold

Needs the toggle above. A handful of the table's prizes target a spell/potion this app has no record of (likely 1st-edition-only) — this swaps those for a flat {{ EDITION1_LOOT_GOLD_VALUE }}gc prize instead, at the point the kill is recorded.

Other supplements
Pentangle schools playable (The Maze of Malcor)

Its five lost schools are scroll-only by the book; this is the optional rule for running them as real schools. Only affects newly created warbands — a wizard's own school is fixed once chosen.

Ragged Warbands & Random Recruits (The Red King)

Adds a "Fill roster" control to the Soldiers card below, rolling random recruits up to 10 members and bypassing the usual gold/roster caps, per the book's endgame campaign mode — plus a "hire for free" picker and a dice-roll entry for adding a recruit you already rolled at the table by hand. The surrounding restrictions (no spending gold, no hiring/dismissing outside this roll, no Vault storage, a soldier lost on a 1-8 survival roll) are shown there as reminder text. Forces "Ordinary soldiers can take permanent injuries" on below, since the Random Recruit Status Table and the book's own Survival Roll can both inflict one on a soldier.

Supplies & Cargo Transports (The Wildwoods)

Adds a Supply Points (sp) economy for wilderness campaigns and a purchasable Cargo Transport to the Wizard & apprentice card below: buy/sell sp (1gc/sp, 2sp/gc), settle post-scenario consumption, and fit the transport with upgrades. The transport's on-table push/combat rules are shown as reference text.

Core rule tweaks
Black Market Contacts

Instead of open buying, roll 4x on the Treasure Table after a game and buy only from what comes up — adds a Black Market sub-card to the Treasury, Vault and Workshop card below to record rolls and look up results.

Adjust soldiers to edition 2

Assassin, Demon Hunter, Monk and Mystic Warrior read as costed closer to 1st edition than the rest of the 2e tables. On by default, this bumps their hiring cost (Assassin 100gc, Demon Hunter/Monk/Mystic Warrior 125gc). Applies to future hires only.

Every ordinary soldier gets a free backup dagger

A single dagger costs no item slot. Skips anyone already carrying a hand weapon and a dagger, or two daggers; upgrades a lone dagger to two, and two hand weapons still gets one added.

Disable app mechanics and show all soldiers

Not a rule — an escape hatch. Hire soldier lists and allows hiring every catalog entry, regardless of source book, known spells, wizard state, or vault items. For working around an app oversight, or a house rule this app doesn't model. Roster/specialist/ legendary limits and gold cost still apply.

Creatures get one item slot

Animal companions and constructs normally have no item slot at all. This grants each one exactly one, still restricted to gear that creature can actually use (e.g. Bear Armour on a Bear) — the picker just won't offer anything else there.

Additional Weapons

Javelin, Bladed Staff and Throwing Knife are class-issued or lightly-supported standard weapons that stay available regardless of enabled source books (see the Lexicon) — turn any of them off here if your group doesn't use it.

Roster caps

2e core defaults to {{ MAX_SOLDIERS }} soldiers / {{ MAX_SPECIALISTS }} specialists. Raise these if your group plays with a bigger warband, or if a mechanic (e.g. Fireheart's Projectile Weapon Construct Modification) has pushed an existing warband over the default specialist cap and you'd rather raise the limit than dismiss someone.
