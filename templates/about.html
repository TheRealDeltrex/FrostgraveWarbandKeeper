About

Local multi-page Frostgrave warband creator and maintainer — also runs entirely in the browser. No login; data stays on your PC (or, in the browser version, in this tab).

Features

  • Wizard: name, school, portrait, spells with casting numbers (own/aligned/neutral/opposed penalties)
  • Apprentice: name + portrait; stats/spells auto-derived from the wizard
  • Level-up wizard (stats / learn / improve spell), with reversible level-ups
  • Wizard states: Lich, Beastcrafter, Demonic Pacts
  • Captains: hire or promote a soldier, tricks, level-up
  • Grave mutations for wizard, apprentice, captain, and soldiers
  • Recruit soldiers with cost + total/specialist limits, optional soldier leveling
  • Home base: location and resources
  • Treasury and vault, post-game gold/XP/items entry
  • Per-warband homerules for most of the above
  • PDF roster export and JSON export/import
  • Frostgrave Lexicon: full spell/soldier/bestiary/magic-item reference
  • Name + picture on every character
